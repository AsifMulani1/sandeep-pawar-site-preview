A reader's question

A reader askedSample: final-year engineering student

“I have an offer from a large company and one from a twelve-person startup. Everyone I ask tells me something different. How should I decide?”

Start with what you want to be able to do in three years, not with which company sounds better today. A large company usually teaches process and gives you structured training. A small one usually gives you more responsibility, sooner, with less support.

Neither is better in general. Ask each one what a person in your role did last month, and pick the month you would rather have lived.
