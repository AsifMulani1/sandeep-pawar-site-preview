Sandeep Pawar is Chief Project Officer at GEMS Education India. Over two decades he has built education brands, led teams and delivered projects.

This site is where he shares what that work taught him, for students, professionals and founders.

Why I write

Most advice on careers, leadership and starting a business arrives as slogans: follow your passion, trust your gut, fail fast. It sounds right and helps little when you have a real decision in front of you.

I write the specific version: what to look for in a first job, how to grow into leading people, what to check before you build something of your own. If you have a question, ask it. Many of the best pieces here start with one.
